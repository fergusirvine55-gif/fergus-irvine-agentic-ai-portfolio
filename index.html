Client Book dashboards

Four single-file browser dashboards for a securities-finance client book: the master Client Book Dashboard & Deck Builder, its client-facing release, an EMEA aged-receivables tracker and a no-install exposure explorer. No server, no install, no network call. Every byte of parsing, charting, statistics and PDF or PPTX export runs inside the browser on a locked-down bank desktop.

Proof Three releases deployed globally. Master build v41 scores a client against peers on 13 similarity factors, answers five analyst questions on an explore surface, runs paired t-tests, an F-test and Granger-style lead-lag tests with Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment, and exports decks into the user's own PPTX template. Every demo figure was recomputed in Python and matched in the browser to the cent.

Merck & Co / Pandion Therapeutics acquisition model

A full M&A model of Merck's $1.85bn acquisition of Pandion Therapeutics, rebuilt from SEC filings and press releases: rNPV, sum of parts, comparables, precedents, football field, sources and uses, ASC 805 purchase price allocation, accretion and dilution, pro forma, synergies, sensitivities and scenarios.

Proof 17 integrated tabs, 751 live formulas, zero errors, plus a six-slide deal book and a five-page methodology guide. The workbook was built by hand; the documents were AI-assisted. Public information only, not investment advice, not affiliated with either company.

Systematic trading pipeline in five gated stages: hypothesis, signal research, build, paper incubation, live.

Proof 196 tests. Ranked probability score 0.2018 on unseen data across 15 leagues and 34,903 matches: a skilful model, and its own conclusion is that no exploitable edge exists against the betting market.

How I build with Claude

  • A Contabo VPS (12 vCPU, 47 GB RAM) runs headless Claude Code for agent fleets. The Mac stays master; cloud sessions take the overnight research.
  • A hook enforces a launch card before any agent run: three configurations, every model and effort level chosen fresh, usage share and finishing time stated. Nothing launches until I type go.
  • Finders write to disk, an independent verifier re-checks their claims, one judge decides. Briefs are files with an output schema.
  • Suites of 196, 129 and 115 tests; hook-enforced stage gates; a kill scoreboard; pre-registration and held-out controls on the models.
  • Single-file HTML tools for bank desktops, Python pipelines, React and Express on Render, artifacts with shared databases and per-user state.
  • One branch per task, draft PRs with a stated merge order, decision ids and version tags, HANDOVER and STATE files in every project.
